## [1.0.0] - 2025-08-23

### Added

Initial public release of the "Basic Jingle Pack" (1.0.0).

- 25+ jingles and sound cues across these categories:
  - GenericJingles (short music stings)
  - GoalHorn (goal horn sounds)
  - GoalJingles (goal celebration jingles)
  - PenaltyJingles (short cues for penalty situations)
  - SpecialJingles (team/timeout voice cues, background loops)
